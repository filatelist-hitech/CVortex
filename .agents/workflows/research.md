# Research Workflow

## READ

Understand the decision that research must support.

## DEFINE QUESTION

State:

- research question;
- why it matters;
- decision affected;
- freshness requirements.

## COLLECT SOURCES

Prefer official and primary sources.

## EXTRACT

For each material finding record:

- source;
- URL;
- publication/update date;
- finding;
- evidence;
- confidence.

## COMPARE

Record conflicting evidence and alternatives.

## INFER

Clearly separate inference from sourced facts.

## DECISION IMPACT

Explain what project decision the finding affects.

## REVIEW DATE

Add a review date when information is likely to become stale.

## COMPLETE

Save the research artifact and update relevant documentation/state.
