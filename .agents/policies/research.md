# Research Policy

## Purpose

Ensure architecture and implementation decisions rely on current evidence rather than assumptions.

## Mandatory Rules

Prefer sources in this order:

1. official documentation;
2. standards;
3. primary sources/research;
4. vendor documentation;
5. high-quality engineering sources;
6. community sources as secondary evidence.

Record:

- research date;
- source URL;
- publication/update date when available;
- finding;
- evidence;
- confidence;
- alternatives;
- decision impact;
- review date when freshness matters.

Clearly distinguish fact from inference.

## Prohibited Behavior

- Inventing APIs or vendor capabilities.
- Guessing current model catalog or pricing.
- Treating SEO articles as authoritative evidence.
- Omitting source URLs.
- Presenting inference as verified fact.
- Hiding conflicting sources.

## Completion Checks

- Material findings have citations.
- Source quality is appropriate.
- Freshness was considered.
- Fact and inference are separated.
- Alternatives are recorded.
- Decision impact is explicit.
