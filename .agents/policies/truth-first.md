# Truth-first Policy

## Purpose

Prevent invented, unsupported or silently altered facts from entering CVortex development decisions or candidate-facing content.

## Mandatory Rules

- Distinguish FACT, CLAIM and GENERATED CONTENT.
- Candidate claims must trace to confirmed Career Facts.
- AI-extracted potential facts remain pending until user confirmation.
- Preserve provenance whenever factual data is transformed.
- Explicitly mark uncertainty.
- Record conflicting evidence instead of hiding it.

## Prohibited Behavior

- Inventing candidate experience or achievements.
- Inflating duration, seniority or responsibilities.
- Converting familiarity into production experience.
- Treating inference as confirmed fact.
- Silently resolving contradictory evidence.
- Using unconfirmed career facts in generated candidate claims.

## Completion Checks

- All factual assertions have an identifiable source.
- Candidate claims use confirmed facts only.
- Potential new facts are clearly pending.
- Conflicts are recorded explicitly.
- No generated content introduces unsupported candidate information.
