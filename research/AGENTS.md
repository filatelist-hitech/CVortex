# Research Scoped Instructions

These instructions apply to files under `research/`.

## Source Priority

Prefer:

1. official documentation;
2. standards;
3. primary sources;
4. vendor documentation;
5. high-quality engineering sources;
6. community sources as supplementary evidence.

## Required Metadata

Research artifacts must preserve, where applicable:

- research date;
- URL;
- publication/update date;
- finding;
- evidence;
- confidence;
- alternatives;
- decision impact;
- review date.

## Fact vs Inference

Clearly distinguish:

- verified/source-supported facts;
- interpretation;
- inference;
- recommendation.

Do not present inference as sourced fact.

## Conflicting Sources

Do not silently reconcile contradictory evidence.

Record:

- what conflicts;
- which sources disagree;
- confidence;
- project impact.

## Freshness

For rapidly changing topics such as:

- LLM models;
- pricing;
- APIs;
- library compatibility;
- vendor capabilities;
- external service policies;

record a review date or freshness requirement.
