# Employer Type Comparison — Fintech / Startups / Enterprise

## Finding

Phase 04 did **not** find sufficient strong independent evidence to justify stable universal rules such as:

- “startups always hire faster”;
- “enterprise always has more interview rounds”;
- “fintech always requires background/security screening”;
- “startups do not use ATS”;
- “enterprise values keywords more than skills”.

Those claims are highly company-, role-, country- and maturity-dependent.

## What is supported

Different employers demonstrably use different hiring tooling and stages. Large platforms/vendors document formal screening, assessment and multi-stage workflows, while individual job postings and company hiring pages can define their own process.

This supports **company-specific Employer Research** more strongly than a rigid employer-type stereotype.

## Provisional dimensions to research per employer

Instead of encoding generic mythology, CVortex should collect evidence for:

- hiring velocity / stated timeline;
- formalization of stages;
- assessment types;
- technical depth;
- ATS/vendor in use when observable and permitted;
- resume/application requirements;
- compliance/security/background requirements;
- work authorization/location constraints;
- salary transparency;
- recruiter vs hiring-manager touchpoints.

## Fintech

Potential relevance of compliance/security/background requirements is plausible but NOT UNIVERSALLY CONFIRMED in this Phase. Treat as company/jurisdiction-specific until evidence exists.

Confidence: LOW.

## Startups

Potentially shorter or less formal hiring processes are common industry anecdotes but were not established strongly enough here to become a Recruitment KB rule.

Confidence: LOW.

## Enterprise

Large-company examples such as Microsoft and Amazon show formal multi-stage technical processes, but two companies cannot establish a universal enterprise rule.

Confidence: LOW/MEDIUM for examples, LOW for generalization.

## Recommendation candidate

Store `employer_type` as a research dimension/filter, not as a deterministic rule source. Prefer employer-specific evidence over a generic type prior. Phase 05 should not freeze behavior that assumes hiring process from company category alone.
