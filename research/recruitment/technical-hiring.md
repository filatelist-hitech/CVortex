# Technical Hiring

## Core finding

Technical hiring is role- and employer-specific. The research supports common stage families, not one universal sequence.

## Evidence

### Microsoft

Microsoft states that technical interviews focus on problem solving and role-specific technical skills; engineering interviews may evaluate design, coding and testing, and interview steps vary by role.

Sources:
- https://careers.microsoft.com/v2/global/en/hiring-tips/technical-interviewing
- https://careers.microsoft.com/v2/global/en/hiring-tips/interview-tips.html

### Amazon SDE II

Amazon documents a concrete sequence for SDE II: application, online assessment, interview loop and outcome. The OA includes coding/system-design/work-style components; the loop combines technical and behavioral assessment.

Source: https://www.amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/sde-ii-interview-prep

## Common stage families

The following are sufficiently evidenced as common **families**, not mandatory stages:

- recruiter/initial screen;
- hiring manager screen;
- technical interview;
- coding or role-specific exercise;
- QA/testing exercise for relevant roles;
- system/design discussion for applicable engineering roles;
- take-home or live exercise at some employers;
- behavioral interview;
- reference/background stages where role/company/jurisdiction requires them;
- final decision.

## QA / automation roles

Direct primary evidence across many employers was limited. Based on technical-hiring sources and role nature, CVortex should model QA-specific preparation as role-scoped rather than pretend the SDE interview template applies unchanged.

Potential categories for later evidence expansion:

- test design and risk analysis;
- API/integration debugging;
- SQL/data validation;
- automation coding;
- CI/CD and flaky test analysis;
- system architecture understanding;
- quality strategy and communication.

These categories are product hypotheses until broader employer evidence is collected.

## Recommendation candidate

Interview preparation should be generated from:

```text
actual vacancy requirements
+ submitted ResumeVersion
+ cover letter / claims used
+ Employer Memory
+ company-specific documented interview information
+ market/role research
```

Do not present Amazon/Microsoft processes as universal templates.
